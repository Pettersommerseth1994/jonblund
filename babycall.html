Step one

Tell it who to call.

Pick a contact, or type a number once. Jon Blund keeps it on this phone, so next time it is a single tap.

Step two

Leave this phone by the baby.

Turn on the microphone and set it down near the crib. Screen on, plugged in. That is the whole setup.

Step three

It listens for crying.

Not the fan, not the traffic outside. Only a sound that keeps going long enough to be a real cry.

Step four

Then your phone rings.

A real call, so it cuts through silent mode. Answer it and you hear the room. Hang up, and Jon Blund goes straight back to listening.
